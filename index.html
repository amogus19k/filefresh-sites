The sync succeeded.
The data still looks wrong.

A successful sync only tells you that a process completed. It does not necessarily tell you:

  • Whether the supplier sent complete data
  • Whether the source structure changed
  • Whether mapping behaved differently
  • Whether a field disappeared during transformation
  • Where an incorrect downstream value first appeared

Seeing that something is wrong is one problem.
Understanding where it became wrong is another.

Supplier data rarely follows a simple path.

Modern commerce teams often depend on several independent tools and suppliers — each with its own logs, formats and definition of “done”.

Many tools, many owners. Suppliers, importers, middleware and destinations are often run by different teams or vendors.

Every step says “success”. Each system may correctly report that its own step succeeded while the final data is still unexpected.

The full path is the gap. FileFresh is being explored as a way to understand the complete path without replacing every system in it.

A clearer view between source and destination.

FileFresh is being designed around a simple idea: capture enough context around the data pipeline to make investigation easier when something looks wrong.

Instead of immediately replacing the importer or sync system, the goal is to help answer better questions.

What arrived?

What did the supplier actually send?

over time

Keep your existing importer.

FileFresh is not intended to become another catalog importer. Your existing workflow may already handle synchronization very well.

The product direction is to make it easier to investigate what happened when the result does not match expectations.

FAQ

FileFresh is early. If your question isn’t here, ask us directly.

What is FileFresh?

FileFresh is an early-stage product being explored around visibility and traceability in supplier-data workflows.

Is FileFresh another feed importer?

No. The current direction is to work alongside existing import and synchronization systems rather than replace them.

What types of data are you looking at?

We’re currently researching workflows involving XML, CSV, APIs, FTP/SFTP, inventory feeds and similar supplier-data sources.

Does FileFresh automatically fix bad feeds?

No. The current focus is understanding the visibility and investigation problem first.

Is FileFresh available today?

We’re currently in customer discovery and early product development. If supplier data is operationally important to your business, we’d like to talk.

Do I need to replace my existing integration?

That is not the current product direction. FileFresh is being explored as a complementary layer around existing workflows.
